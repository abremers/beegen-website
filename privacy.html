Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

Beegen ("we", "us", "our") operates beegen.co and app.beegen.co. We provide AI-powered tools for local businesses, including a website builder and a WhatsApp AI assistant with appointment booking. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@beegen.co.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information (email address, password) when you sign up
  • Prompts and instructions you send to the AI builder
  • Brand assets you upload (logos, colour preferences)
  • Payment information processed securely via our payment provider

We also collect usage data automatically, including pages visited, features used, and device/browser information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Beegen service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, account notices)
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. AI Processing

Your prompts are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate website content. Anthropic's own privacy policy governs their handling of API data. We do not use your prompts or generated sites to train our own AI models.

5. Google API Services and Calendar Data

Beegen integrates with Google Calendar to power the appointment booking feature in our WhatsApp AI product. When you choose to connect your Google Calendar:

  • What we access: We read your calendar's free/busy availability and create new calendar events when a customer books an appointment.
  • How we use it: Solely to check open time slots before offering them to customers via WhatsApp, and to create a calendar event when a booking is confirmed. We do not access, store, or use your Google Calendar data for any other purpose.
  • How we share it: We do not share your Google Calendar data with any third parties.
  • How to disconnect: You can revoke Beegen's access to your Google Calendar at any time from your dashboard (WhatsApp AI → Citas → Desconectar). You can also remove access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Compliance: Beegen's use of data obtained via Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account yourself from the dashboard (Settings → Delete my account) or request deletion at any time by emailing privacy@beegen.co. Full instructions: beegen.co/data-deletion.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at hello@beegen.co to exercise these rights.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes by email or via the app.
